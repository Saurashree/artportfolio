About the Artist

Hi, I'm Saurashree!

I am a Class VII student at Bhavan's Gangabux Kanoria Vidyamandir, where I balance my academics with a wonderfully diverse set of hobbies. I have a strong creative streak, enjoying both art and crafts as well as crochet.

My artistic side extends to singing and writing stories, while my inquisitive mind keeps me engaged in reading and solving puzzles and riddles. With such a rich blend of creative, intellectual, and artistic pursuits, I come across as a curious and imaginative young student with a lot of varied interests.
